# Ablation Study Summary: ROI-Weighted vs Unweighted MIL

**Objective**: Evaluate whether integrating pixel-level ROI information (from U-Net) into attention-based MIL improves slide-level HER2 classification compared to unweighted aggregation.

**Test Set**: 90 slides (limited for fast evaluation)

**Approach**: 
- Use pre-trained MIL models (trained with ROI weighting)
- Compare inference with and without ROI modulation
- NO retraining - inference-only modification
